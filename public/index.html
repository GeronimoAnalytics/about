About

Most data people own one layer of the stack. I build the whole chain, from the Fabric pipeline to the pixel your board looks at, and lately the AI agent that answers their follow-up question.

As a driven data analyst and Power BI developer, I believe that teamwork, organization, and flexibility are the key ingredients for success. My core skills include data analysis, data visualization engineering, and data discovery and modeling, using AI where it has its place. While business decisions can often be based on gut feeling, why not back them up with data?

Data only drives action when it connects with human cognition. I design data visualizations based on cognitive design principles, making insights instinctual, decisions faster, and impact bigger.
